About Echoes of the Past

Echoes of the Past is an educational 3D quest-based mobile game designed to introduce players to significant people, places, and events in Philippine history.

Through interactive exploration, historical storytelling, artifact discovery, and knowledge-based quizzes, players can learn about the country's rich historical heritage in an engaging environment.

This support page serves as a dedicated resource for players who encounter difficulties, need assistance, or wish to provide feedback about the game.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Echoes of the Past.

What is Echoes of the Past?

Echoes of the Past is a 3D educational mobile game that allows players to explore Philippine history through interactive quests, historical locations, and quizzes.

What devices support the game?

The game is designed for Android mobile devices. Performance may vary depending on your device specifications and Android version.

How do I start a new adventure?

Open the game and select "Start New Game" from the Main Menu. This will begin a new historical journey.

How can I continue my saved game?

Select "Continue Game" from the Main Menu to resume your previously saved progress. Please note that starting a new game may replace your existing progress.

How do I complete a historical quest?

Follow the assigned objectives, including learning about historical figures, visiting historical locations, finding artifacts, and answering quizzes. You must correctly answer at least 7 out of 10 questions to proceed.

Does the game require an internet connection?

The game is designed to store player progress locally on the device and does not require a cloud save account.

What should I do if the game crashes?

Try restarting the application and checking your device's available storage. If the problem continues, submit a support request describing the issue and your Android device model.

How can I report incorrect historical information?

Select "Historical Content Concern" in the support form and describe the information you believe requires correction or verification.
